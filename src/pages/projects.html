Agent Research Assistant

A research assistant concept powered by LLM agents, designed for deeper, structured exploration.

Problem space

Research questions usually need many sources, steps and checks — more than a single prompt can hold.

Approach

Break the task into agent steps — plan, gather, read, summarise — keeping each step inspectable.

Focus areas

Tool use, structured outputs and keeping a human in the loop.
